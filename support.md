# Vesta support

Vesta is a chat app for running a home: the family and the people who work in
the home share chats, a grocery list and tasks, in their own languages.

## Contact

Email **[vesta.app.help@gmail.com](mailto:vesta.app.help@gmail.com)**. We
usually reply within two working days. Please include the phone number you
sign in with and, if something went wrong, a screenshot.

You can also write to us from inside the app: **Settings → Send feedback**.

## Common questions

**I didn't get my sign-in code.** Codes come on WhatsApp, or by SMS if WhatsApp
is not set up. Wait a minute, check your number (with the country code) and
tap Resend.

**How do I add my cook, driver or cleaner?** Settings → Invite. They sign in
with the number you entered and join your home straight away.

**How do I pay for Vesta Plus?** Settings → Vesta Plus. Payment goes through
Apple or Google. To cancel, use your phone's subscription settings (on iPhone:
Settings → your name → Subscriptions; on Android: Play Store → Payments &
subscriptions). When a plan ends, nothing is deleted.

**How do I delete my account?** In the app: Settings → Delete my account. If
you cannot open the app, email us from any address with the phone number you
signed in with, and we will delete it within 7 days.
