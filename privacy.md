# Vesta privacy policy

_Last updated: 28 October 2026_

Vesta is a chat app for running a home: the family and the people who work in
the home (cook, driver, cleaners and others) share chats, a grocery list and
tasks. This policy says what Vesta collects, why, who helps us run it, and how
you delete it. Vesta is made and run by Samir Bagasrawala, an individual developer in
Mumbai, India ("we"). Contact: vesta.app.help@gmail.com.

## What we collect

- **Your phone number.** You sign in with it. We send a one-time code to it on
  WhatsApp or by SMS.
- **Your name and language**, as you or your home's admin enter them.
- **Phone numbers of people you invite**, with the name and role you give
  them, so they can join your home when they sign in. If they never join, the
  invite expires.
- **What you send in your home's chats:** messages, voice notes, photos, files
  and links, and reactions.
- **What Vesta makes from them:** the grocery list, tasks, recipes, reminders,
  and the "running out soon" estimates on the Insights tab.
- **Places**, if you add them: your home's location and places the driver goes.
  Vesta asks for your phone's location only while you are pinning a place.
- **Travel details**, if you share a ticket or boarding pass, so Vesta can
  plan the airport or station drop.
- **Staff pay and attendance**, if the family uses them: the monthly pay and
  payments they record, days present or absent, and leave requests. Only the
  family and that staff member see them.
- **Notification tokens**, so your phone can get Vesta notifications.
- **Feedback** you send from Settings → Send feedback, with your home's name,
  the app version and your phone type.
- **Which tabs are used**: once a day per tab, counted per home and by family
  or staff, never what is on the screen.
- **Crash reports**: what went wrong, the phone model and app version. No
  message text and no name or phone number.
- **Vesta Plus purchases**: whether you have Plus and until when. Apple or
  Google takes the payment; we never see your card or UPI details. When a
  plan ends, the home locks but nothing is deleted.
- **Delivery app sign-ins**, only if an owner connects a delivery app on the
  Order online screen. These stay on our server and are never shown in the app.

Vesta asks before using your microphone (voice notes), camera and photos
(sending pictures), location (only while pinning a place) and calendar (adding
trips). Calendar entries stay on your phone.

If you use **Siri** with Vesta, Apple turns what you say into text on its side
first; Vesta then gets that text as a message from you, like anything you type.

We do not collect contacts, we never show ads, and we do not sell or rent your
information to anyone.

## Why we use it

Only to run Vesta for your home: to sign you in, deliver messages, turn
messages and voice notes into list items and tasks, translate items and tasks
into each person's language, send reminders and notifications, and plan trips.

Translations of everyday item names (for example "Onion" to "प्याज़") are
reused between homes, so each word is translated once. Only the item's name
and its translation are reused, never who asked for it, your messages or
anything about your home.

## Who can see it

- **People in your home** see the chats they are in. Staff see only their own
  chats, the list if their role allows it, and their own tasks. Only the family
  sees Insights.
- **Services that help run Vesta**, each only for its part:
  - Supabase (database, file storage and sign-in; data stored in Mumbai, India)
  - Anthropic (Claude AI reads chat text to make list items, tasks and
    translations)
  - Sarvam AI (turns voice notes into text), and Groq for voice notes in
    Arabic, Tagalog, Sinhala and Indonesian
  - Google Maps (place search and drive times)
  - YouTube (the title and description of a recipe video someone shares).
    For other shared links, our server opens the link once to show its title
    and picture, as most chat apps do.
  - Meta WhatsApp and our SMS provider (sign-in codes)
  - Expo, Apple and Google (delivering notifications)
  - Sentry (crash reports)
  - RevenueCat (keeps track of Vesta Plus purchases made through Apple and
    Google)
  - Delivery apps you choose to connect (Zepto, Swiggy Instamart), which get
    the items you send to their basket

  These services may only use your information to provide their service to us.
  Anthropic does not use it to train its models.
- **The law.** We share information if Indian law requires it.

## How long we keep it

As long as your account is open. Sign-in codes are deleted within a day.
Messages stay in your home's chats until the home is deleted or you delete your
account (see below).

## Agreeing, and when you leave a job

Each person sees a short "Before you start" page in their own language when
they join a home, and agrees for themselves. When someone stops working in a
home, the home's admin can remove them, and can also erase every message,
photo and voice note they sent there.

## Deleting your account

In Vesta: **Settings → Delete my account**. This deletes your sign-in, phone
number, name, language and notification settings straight away.

- Messages you sent stay in your home's chats, shown as "Deleted account", so
  the conversation still makes sense for the others.
- If you were the last family member of a home, that home is deleted too, with
  all its chats, photos, voice notes, list and tasks.

If you cannot open the app, email vesta.app.help@gmail.com from any address with the
phone number you signed in with, and we will delete the account within 7 days.

## Your rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 you can ask us to see,
correct or delete your information, and to name someone to act for you. Write
to vesta.app.help@gmail.com. If we do not resolve your concern you can complain to the
Data Protection Board of India.

## Children

Vesta accounts are for adults. Children may appear in a home's messages
(for example, a school pickup task), but they do not get their own accounts.

## How we protect your chats

- Everything travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS), and is stored
  encrypted on Supabase's servers in Mumbai.
- Each home's data is walled off from every other home in the database, and
  staff see only what their role allows.
- Vesta's AI (Claude, by Anthropic) reads a home's chat text only to keep its
  list and tasks. Anthropic does not use it to train its models.
- We do not read your chats. We look at a home's data only if you ask us to
  (for example, to fix a problem you report) or if Indian law requires it.
- Chats are not end-to-end encrypted: Vesta's AI has to read the text to turn
  it into your list and tasks. We say this plainly so you can decide what to
  write.

## Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above and tell you in the app
if the change matters.
