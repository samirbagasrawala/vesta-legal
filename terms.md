## What Vesta is

Vesta is a chat app for running a home. The family and the people who work in
the home share chats, a grocery list and tasks. Vesta's AI reads the home's
chats, voice notes and photos to keep the list and tasks up to date and to
translate between languages. The AI can make mistakes, so check anything
important (amounts, medicines, payments) yourself.
